Ends at 60 minutes

Run Together comes first, before Pressure. Turning somebody is only worth anything if you can then run with them — the turn ends shoulder to shoulder, which is exactly what Run Together teaches. Teach it second and the turn has nowhere to go. The chain reads the other way because that is the order it happens in a game, not the order to learn it.

Key Concepts

Two things to say all hour. They are not the lesson — they are what to fall back on when a drill starts falling apart.

The whole day“You don’t have to win the ball.”

Slowing them down is the job. Every drill today scores it that way, and a nine-year-old who believes it stops diving in.

Whenever two defenders are near the ball“One goes. One backs up.”

The closest one pressures. The other drops behind them, not beside them, so there is still somebody there if the first one gets beaten. If both go, one dribble takes out two defenders.

1

Warm-up

  • Everyone with a ball in the box, dribbling, from the minute they arrive.
  • Shout “Space!” when they bunch up.
  • Last minute: “Freeze!” — foot on the ball, look up, find a gap, go again.
2

Drill — Learn Names

Drill · Learn Names 4 minutes

Overview

Full rules in the drill
  1. Same box, keep dribbling. No new setup.
  2. Coach calls a name — everybody dribbles to that player.
  3. Shout “Space!” to start the next round.

Second time out, so run it short. If they have it: the player who was called calls the next name.

3

Lesson — Run Together

Lesson · 03 — One on One Defense, third beat 2 minutes

They got past you, or you both went for a loose ball. Now you are running side by side.

Reinforce these two today

Getting alongside“Go where they go.”

Shoulder to shoulder, one step ahead. You are not trying to win it yet, just not letting them get away.

What the ref allows“Shoulder to shoulder is soccer. Hands are a foul.”
4

Drill — Shoulder Race

Drill · Shoulder Race 7 minutes Reinforces · lesson 03

Overview

Full rules in the drill
  1. Setup — two queues at one end of a field line, two cones about 20 ft along it.
  2. Front pair both step onto the same line and go on the call.
  3. Walk, no ball. Each holds one end of a penny in their outside hand so nobody can drift away. Be the person in front at the finish.
  4. Stop and fix whatever went wrong. Walk it again, penny still in.
  5. Drop the penny. Walk with a ball. Then run, and swim past.

“Glued at the hip!” — “Swim in front!”

Walking is not a warm-up round — you cannot barge at walking pace, so it is the round that teaches the shoulder.

5

Lesson — Pressure & Turn Them

Lesson · 03 — One on One Defense, first two beats 2 minutes

No equipment, no ball. Stand them in a half circle, take one player, and walk at them twice. This is their first time seeing it — it does not need to be good today.

Four takeaways

Demo, twice
  1. Get to one side of them — not straight in front.
  2. Force them one way. Shut one side, leave the other open.
  3. Turn your body so you are ready to run with them when they go.
  4. When you get the ball, dribble it away — don’t kick or poke it.

“Direct traffic!”

Do it wrong first — stand square and let them stroll past either side — then do it right. The wrong one is what they remember. Number three is why Run Together came first: turning your body is how you end up already running with them instead of chasing from behind.

6

Drill — 1v1

Drill · One Way Out 13 minutes Reinforces · lesson 03

Overview

Full rules in the drill
  1. Setup — a 12-yd box with three cone goals: one at the far end for the attacker, one on each side for the defender. Two boxes with ten players, five at each.
  2. Attacker comes in at one end. Attacker wins through the far goal.
  3. Defender wins by taking the ball and dribbling through either side goal. A kick through does not count.
  4. Round one: no tackling at all — only stand in the way.
  5. After your go, the attacker becomes the defender and the defender queues.

“Which gate is closed?”

Straight down the middle means the defender was square — they gave them both ways. Nobody has to be told.

7

Lesson — Defense Partners

Lesson · 04 — Defense Partners, not written up yet 2 minutes

Everything so far was one defender. Now there are two, and the second one has a different job from the first.

Reinforce these two today

The whole idea“Closest pressures. The other backs up.”

The second defender stands behind the first, not beside them. If both go to the ball, one dribble beats both of them at once.

Out loud“I got ball!” — “I got back!”

Two defenders who say nothing both go, or neither does.

8

Drill — 2v1

Drill · 2v1 in the same box, not written up yet 10 minutes Reinforces · lesson 04

Same box, same goals, a second defender steps in. Nothing to move, so it runs straight on from the 1v1. Go to 2v2 only if there is time and the shape is holding — it is the harder version, not the real one.

Overview

Whole team10 min
  1. Setup — the 1v1 boxes, unchanged. Two defenders in each instead of one.
  2. Attackers come in one at a time, every 8 seconds. Waiting attackers keep dribbling — nobody stands still.
  3. Same scoring: attacker through the far goal, defenders dribble out a side goal.
  4. One goes, one backs up. The second defender is behind the first, not beside.
  5. After four attackers, the defenders swap out and the next two go in.

“Who pressures? Who backs up?”

Do not worry yet whether the attacker scores. Stop for five seconds and praise the shape: “Perfect — you went, and you were behind helping.” If they have it, send attackers in pairs: now it is 2v2 and the back defender has a real decision.

9

Scrimmage

If time permits

Split into two teams. Today you are watching defenders, not goals. This is the block that gets cut — if the 2v1 is working, stay in it.

What you shout

“Stop short!” “Turn them!” “Run together!” “One goes, one backs up!”

Nothing else. Every one came from a block above, and a phrase they have not been taught is just noise from the sideline.
