Key Concepts

Two things to say all hour. They are not the lesson — they are what to fall back on when a drill starts falling apart.

The whole day“You don’t have to win the ball.”

Slowing them down is the job. Every drill today scores it that way, and a nine-year-old who believes this stops diving in.

Whenever there are two of them“Never both at the ball.”

One goes, one is behind helping. Say it in the gate drill and keep saying it in the scrimmage.

Warm-up

  • Everyone with a ball in the box, dribbling, from the minute they arrive.
  • Shout “Space!” when they bunch up.
  • Last minute: coach calls “Freeze!” — foot on the ball, look up, find a gap, go again.
2

Drill — Learn Names

Drill · Learn Names 4 minutes

Overview

Full rules in the drill
  1. Keep dribbling in the same box. No new setup.
  2. Coach calls a name — everybody dribbles to that player.
  3. Shout “Space!” to start the next round.

Second time out, so run the short version. If they have it, use the harder one: the player who was called calls the next name.

3

Pressure & Turn Them

Lesson · 03 — One on One Defense 14 minutes

The first two beats only. Run Together is the next block — do not teach all three at once.

Reinforce these three today

Getting there“Fast to get there. But stop short.”

Stopping short gives us space and time to keep up and force a mistake.

Your feet“Scooter stance.”

One foot in front, one behind. Show them only one shoulder.

Which way“Send them to the sideline.”

Block off one path. Give them only one way to go — away from the middle.

Drill — The 6-Second Wall

Whole team10 min
  1. Setup — 3 or 4 channels side by side, 12 yds long, 6 yds wide. Everyone in a channel, nobody watching.
  2. Attacker with a ball at one end, defender at the other.
  3. The defender is not trying to win the ball. Stop the attacker crossing the end line for 6 seconds. Count it out loud.
  4. Attacker crosses → attacker’s point. Defender survives → defender’s point.
  5. Swap every rep. Play to 3, then find a new partner.

“Round 1 — walking only, no ball.”

That round is the one that teaches it: a walking defender cannot stab, so the only thing that works is arriving under control, getting sideways, and staying in front. Round 2 the defender may jog. Round 3 live.

4

Run Together

Lesson · 03 — One on One Defense, third beat 9 minutes

They got past you, or you both went for a loose ball. Now you are running side by side.

Reinforce these two today

Getting alongside“Go where they go.”

Shoulder to shoulder, one step ahead. You are not trying to win it yet, just not letting them get away.

What the ref allows“Shoulder to shoulder is soccer. Hands are a foul.”

Drill — Shoulder to Shoulder

Whole team7 min
  1. Setup — pairs side by side on a line, one cone 12 yds away.
  2. Coach rolls a ball toward the cone. Both players go.
  3. Round 1 walking, friendly — both get there, nobody wins. Practise the shoulder.
  4. Round 2 walking, competitive. Round 3 running.
  5. Then move the cone off to one side, so they have to cut the angle.

“Shoulder to shoulder. No hands.”

Hands means the other player gets the point. That is the whole refereeing.

5

Helping A Defender

Lesson · 04 — Defense Partners, not written up yet 9 minutes

Everything above was one defender. Now there are two, and the second one has a different job from the first.

Reinforce these two today

The whole idea“Closest pressures. The other backs up.”

Never both at the ball. The second defender stands behind the first, not beside them.

Out loud“I got ball!” — “I got back!”

Two defenders who say nothing both go, or neither does.

Drill — Partners at the Gate

Adult #1Adult #27 min
  1. Setup — two stations, one adult each, team split in half.
  2. Each station: a 3-yd gate of two cones, two defenders protecting it.
  3. Everyone else is an attacker with a ball, spread out about 15 yds away.
  4. Attackers go one at a time, every 8 seconds, and try to dribble through the gate. Waiting attackers keep dribbling — nobody stands still.
  5. After four attackers, the two defenders join the attackers and the next two go in.

“Who pressures? Who backs up?”

Do not worry yet whether the attacker scores. Stop for five seconds and praise the shape: “Perfect — you went, and you were behind helping.” If they have it, send the attackers in pairs: now it is 2v2 and the back defender has a real decision.

6

Scrimmage

Split into two teams. Today you are watching defenders, not goals.

What you shout

“Stop short!” “Turn them!” “Run together!” “Never both at the ball!”

Nothing else. Every one of these came from a block above, and a phrase they have not been taught is just noise from the sideline.
