Coaching rule: explain for no more than 90 seconds, then get them moving and reinforce the idea while they play.

1

Our Positions

What we tell the players

Forward“Be someone we can pass to.”
Midfield“Move the ball up the field.”
Left“Keep our left side.”
Center“Stay connected.”
Right“Keep our right side.”
Defenders“Protect our goal and help each other.”

Left generally stays left. Right generally stays right.

Goalie“Protect the goal and start our attack.”

Exercise — Find Your Home

Put cones on the field marking the six field positions. Assign everyone a position. Players dribble or move around freely. Then call:

“HOME!”

Everyone gets back to their position as quickly as possible.

Repeat, switch positions, then introduce a ball so they begin seeing how their position relates to the rest of the team.

2

Where Can the Goalie Go?

What we tell the players

The rule“Inside the big box: you can use your hands. Outside the box: you’re a regular soccer player. No hands.”
When defending“Don’t stay glued to the goal line.”

Move forward when the ball is farther away. Move left and right so you stay between the ball and the middle of the goal.

Ready position“Knees bent. Hands ready. Watch the ball.”

Get your body behind the ball.

Remember“Your hands might miss. Your body is your backup.”
When you catch it“Catch → Hug → Look”

Exercise — Goalie Movement & Saves

Adult #1

Start with easy shots. Move the ball to different locations before shooting. The goalkeeper must first move so they are between the ball and the center of the goal. Progress to:

  1. Ball in center → goalie gets set → easy shot
  2. Ball moves left/right → goalie moves with it → shot
  3. Ball farther away → goalie comes forward
  4. Catch → hug the ball
  5. Catch → hug → look for a teammate

Rotate players through goalkeeper.

3

Goalie Gets the Ball — Now What?

What we tell the players

First“Don’t rush. You have time.”
Then“Catch → Hug → LOOK.”

Look at your teammates. Find someone who is open. Whenever possible, start by looking toward the sides of the field, away from the crowd in the middle.

How“Roll it. Throw it. Pass it.”

You don’t need to blast the ball as far as possible.

The whole idea“Give the ball to our team.”

Exercise — Find the Open Player

Adult #1

Place players or cones as passing targets to the left, center, and right. The goalkeeper catches an easy shot. Call or show which player is covered. The goalkeeper must:

  1. Catch
  2. Hug
  3. Look
  4. Find an open player
  5. Distribute accurately

Progress by adding one defender who tries to take away one passing option.

4

Defenders Work Together

What we tell the players

The idea“The two defenders are partners.”
The cue“ONE GOES — ONE BACKS UP.”
Aggressor · closest to the ball“Go challenge the attacker.”

Slow them down and try to win the ball.

Backup · the other one“Get behind your partner and protect the goal.”

If the attacker gets past your partner, you’re there.

Exercise — 2 Defenders vs. 1 Attacker

Adult #2

Set up: one attacker with a ball, two defenders, one goal. The attacker tries to score. The defenders must decide: who goes? who backs up?

Don’t worry initially about whether the attacker scores. Stop briefly and praise the shape:

“Perfect. You’re going to the ball, and you’re behind helping.”

Rotate after each attempt. Once they understand it, progress to 2 attackers vs. 2 defenders.

If we have time

Shooting

What we tell the players

The cue“Look → Pick a corner → Put the ball there.”

Don’t just kick as hard as you can.

Players can pass it into the corner, dribble closer before shooting, or shoot when they have a good opening.

Exercise — Pick Your Corner

Players dribble toward goal. Before shooting, they look up and choose a corner. Start with accuracy rather than power.

Not today’s lesson

Concepts to Introduce Naturally

We do not need to teach all of these today. Use them as coaching cues if the situations appear during play.

When we have the ball“GET BIG.”

Spread out and become someone your teammate can pass to. Wide and forward.

When they have the ball“GET TOGETHER.”

Come back toward our goal and help each other. Back and together.

If you’re lost“FIND YOUR HOME.”

Remember your position and your part of the field.

Scrimmage

Finish with as much 7v7-style soccer as our numbers allow. Don’t constantly stop play. Coach primarily with today’s cues — and let everything else be soccer.

“FIND YOUR HOME!” “KEEP YOUR SIDE!” “ONE GOES — ONE BACKS UP!” “GOALIE — LOOK!” “BE SOMEONE WE CAN PASS TO!”
