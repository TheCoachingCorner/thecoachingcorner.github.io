Key Concepts

Three phrases for the whole hour. They are not a lesson — they come up in every drill and in the scrimmage, and they are what to fall back on when a session starts getting complicated.

When we have the ball“GET BIG.”

Spread out and become someone your teammate can pass to. Wide and forward.

When they have the ball“GET TOGETHER.”

Come back toward our goal and help each other. Back and together.

Any time anyone is lost“FIND YOUR HOME.”

Your position, and your part of the field. This one gets used more than the other two put together today.

Meet Our Team

First practice. Nobody stands in a line.

Names and a ball

Whole team8 min

Set up: a loose box, a ball each. Players arrive and start straight away.

  1. Dribble anywhere in the box. When you pass someone, say your name to them.
  2. Coach calls a name; everybody stops and that player says one thing about themselves.
  3. Last two minutes: coach calls a name, everybody dribbles to that player.

Late arrivals join by picking up a ball. There is nothing to explain to them, which is the point of opening this way.

Lesson — Our Positions

Lesson · 01 — Position Fundamentals 6 minutes

Open the lesson on a phone or hand round the cards. Everything — the diagram, the seven position cards — is in there. Do not re-teach it here.

Reinforce these three today

Everybody“Everybody has a home.”

Six spots on the field and one in goal. Yours is a place, not a person to follow.

Left and right“Left stays left. Right stays right.”

They don’t cross the middle. They defend, or get open for a pass that switches sides.

Midfield“Stay behind the ball.”

The one thing to say to the three in the middle on day one. Constant pressure comes later.

The rest of the lesson — the forward, the defenders, the goalie’s cue — is worth reading, not worth saying today. Three things is the budget.

Drill — Find Your Position

Drill · Find Your Position 12 minutes Reinforces · lesson 01

Cones on the six field positions with a printout at each one. Naming test first, then sharks.

On the grass

Full rules in the drill
  1. Cards face down. Everyone on their cone. Ask each player their position, and to point at where their neighborhood ends.
  2. Cards face up. Two sharks, everyone else with a ball.
  3. A shark who dribbles your ball three times takes it. You yell your position, they yell it back, then you are the shark.
  4. Call “POSITIONS!” Reach your cone and name it to be safe. Sharks keep hunting.

“POSITIONS!” — “Say it back!” — “I can’t hear you!”

First time out, run it with one shark and walk the first round. The drill page has the harder and easier dials.

Lesson — Playing Goalie

Lesson · 02 — Goalie Introduction 6 minutes

Everyone plays goalie this season, so everyone hears this once.

Reinforce these three today

Where hands work“Inside the big box, you can use your hands.”

Outside it you are a regular player. The goalie can go anywhere on the field.

Where to stand“Stand on the line in front of the goal.”

Out of the goal, so shooters have a smaller target.

Once you have it“Hug the ball, then start our attack.”

Catch, hug, look, pass — and pass to the sides, never through the middle.

Drill — Goalie

Drill · not written yet 10 minutes Reinforces · lesson 02

To be written with Ben. Ten minutes, everyone takes a turn in goal, and it has to reach the three things the lesson just taught: hands only inside the big box, standing off the line, and hugging the ball before looking up to pass.

Scrimmage

Split into two teams. Rotate the goalie every couple of minutes so the last ten minutes are still part of the goalie lesson.

What you shout

“GET BIG!” “GET TOGETHER!” “FIND YOUR HOME!”

Only these three. They are the key concepts from the top of this page, and a phrase they have not been taught is just noise from the sideline.
