One Way Out

Serves · Lesson 03 — One on One Defense, the pressure and turn them beats 10 minutes Any even number

Needs · 8 cones per box · a ball per pair. Cones, no nets — nothing to fish a ball out of

introdefense

The big idea

DON’T TACKLE → STEER

Two goals behind you, one behind them. Win it and dribble through yours — a kick through is not a win.

Set Up

A box about 12 yds square with three goals. The attacker comes in at one end and attacks the goal at the far end. The defender has a goal on each side.

Three cone goals and no nets. A ball through any of them rolls on, and the next pair is already going.

How It Runs

Basic 1v1

Whole team10 min
  1. Attacker comes in at one end with the ball.
  2. Attacker wins by dribbling through the goal at the far end.
  3. Defender wins by taking the ball and dribbling through either side goal.
  4. A kick through does not count. Poke it away and the attacker just goes and gets it.
  5. Swap every rep. Play to 3, then find a new partner.

“Direct traffic!”

First round, the defender may not tackle at all — only stand in the way. It takes the stabbing out before it becomes a habit. Let tackling in from round two.

Modifiers

Easier
  • Wider box — more room to be wrong in.
  • Walking only, every round.
  • Defender starts closer to the gate, so there is less space to cover.
Harder
  • Narrow the box, so the angle matters more.
  • Coach names a side before each rep — that is the way the defender must send them.
  • Once the attacker has committed to a side, now the defender may win the ball.

What You Shout

“Dribble it out!” “Direct traffic!” “Stop short!”
Why it works Everything above is what you need on the grass. Below is the thinking behind it — read it once before the session, not during.

Why It Works

Why no tackling “The only tool is where you stand.”

It removes every way a nine-year-old gets hurt in a 1v1, and it is the only way they learn the angle. A child allowed to tackle will always tackle, and never finds out that standing in the right place would have done it for them.

Why dribbling out, not kicking out “A kick is a pass to the other team.”

Winning by kicking it away rewards the exact habit we are trying to remove. Making the defender keep the ball turns a tackle into possession, which is the fourth takeaway of the lesson and the hardest one to get on a Saturday.

The mistake shows itself Straight down the middle means you were square.

You gave them both ways. Nobody has to be told.

Open questions. When to allow tackling: never means they never practise the last part, too early means they go back to stabbing, and round two is the guess. — And box size: too small and the defender wins by standing still, too big and the attacker never gets caught. 12 yds square is a starting point, untested. — Direct traffic beat block traffic because a traffic cop who blocks the road is standing still, and the third takeaway is being turned ready to run. Directing is steering, which is the thing.
